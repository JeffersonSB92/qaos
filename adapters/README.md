# Adapters

Adapters are the translation layer between QAOS methodology and a specific technology, language, framework, or stack.

```text
principles
    ↓
standards
    ↓
adapter
    ↓
project implementation
```

Adapters may include stack-specific guidance, recommended practices, known risks, framework-specific patterns and anti-patterns, tooling notes, scaffolds, and links to official references.

They must not replace global standards, store client-specific rules, or modify QAOS philosophy. Future boilerplate and technical configuration belong in `scaffolds/` within the relevant adapter, not in `templates/`.
