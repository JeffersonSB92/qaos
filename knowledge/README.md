# Knowledge

Knowledge is research and supporting knowledge that informs QAOS but does not directly govern it.

```text
knowledge/
├── inbox/
├── sources/
├── topics/
└── syntheses/
```

## Inbox

Captured material awaiting review.

## Sources

Structured notes about non-official external articles, videos, books, papers, and other sources.

## Topics

Thematic notes that connect multiple sources, learnings, and related concepts.

## Syntheses

Internal conclusions produced after analyzing multiple pieces of evidence.

> Knowledge informs the QAOS but is not normative.

Content in `knowledge/` must not automatically become a standard, pattern, or policy.
